About Inverse Cooking AI

Project Overview

People enjoy food photography because they appreciate culinary art. Behind each meal, there is a rich story and preparation process. However, simply looking at a food photograph does not give us access to its ingredients or cooking steps.

Inverse Cooking AI solves this challenge by predicting ingredients as an unordered set and decoding coherent, step-by-step cooking instructions by attending simultaneously to both the visual image and its inferred ingredients.

Key Capabilities

  • Deep Computer Vision (ResNet-50): Spatial visual feature extraction fine-tuned on Recipe1M.
  • Transformer Instruction Decoder: Autoregressive dual-recipe generation with standard and creative variations.
  • Global & Regional Food Recognition: Hybrid multimodal vision engine supporting local Ghanaian specialties (Banku, Fufu, Jollof, Waakye) and world dishes (Sushi, Ramen, Tacos, Pizza).
