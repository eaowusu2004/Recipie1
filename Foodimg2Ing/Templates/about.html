About Inverse Cooking AI

Project Overview

Inverse Cooking AI is an end-to-end deep learning system that reconstructs cooking recipes directly from food images. Behind every meal there is a story and a preparation process—our application bridges the gap between visual food appreciation and actionable cooking knowledge.

Technical Architecture

Our model employs a multi-stage approach:

  • Computer Vision Encoder (ResNet-50): Extracts high-dimensional visual feature representations from uploaded food photographs.
  • Ingredient Prediction Module: Formulates ingredients as an unordered set, modeling their joint dependencies without imposing an artificial order.
  • Transformer Instruction Decoder: Generates coherent, step-by-step cooking instructions by attending simultaneously to visual image patches and inferred ingredient embeddings.
